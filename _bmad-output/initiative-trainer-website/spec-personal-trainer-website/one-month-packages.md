## Supplied Agreement Policies

- Missed classes cannot be made up, carried over, transferred, or refunded. Unused classes expire at the end of the one-month period.
- One-month packages and class packs are non-transferable. All sales are final; the agreement says no refunds, credits, exchanges, or transfers are issued.
- Package prices are before applicable sales tax unless otherwise stated.
- Clients should arrive 5 to 10 minutes early. A signed liability waiver is required before the first class.
- Clients must follow facility rules and trainer instructions and disclose relevant physical limitations or injuries.
- The trainer may modify exercises, class structure, or the schedule when reasonably necessary and determines available class dates.
